# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in Zeus to be a straightforward, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Things that make this project work:

* being direct about technical disagreements and gracious about the outcome
* giving and accepting review that is specific rather than personal
* saying "I was wrong" plainly and moving on
* assuming the other person is trying to build the same thing you are

Things that are not acceptable:

* sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
* insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling
* public or private harassment
* publishing others' private information without explicit permission
* sustained disruption of discussion

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages — and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at
<mjrafg2@gmail.com>. Reports are handled privately and in confidence.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, code, issues and pull
requests that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain why when they
do. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban
from the project.
