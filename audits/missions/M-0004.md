## Where it stopped, and why

Both spawned tasks ended `NEEDS_RECONCILIATION`. The required `unit-test`
check — this project's own suite — ran for 180 s inside the task scope and was
killed as `TEST_TIMEOUT`. The escalation reads: *the check did not fail, it did
not run*. No change was integrated, the mission ratchet never advanced, and the
mission terminated `PARTIAL / BLOCKED`.

The two criteria that came out `PROVEN` are the two that assert nothing broke,
and they were trivially true because nothing was changed. They are not evidence
of achievement and are not counted as any.

What the run established is on the record either way: the planner, the plan
critic, both consent gates, the live preflight, task spawning, worktree
isolation, the check vocabulary, escalation, final oracle evaluation and
termination all executed against live providers. Integration, the green ratchet
and effect verification did not — nothing reached them.
